02

Passive sensing of gait & medication fluctuations in Parkinson's

Testing whether contact-free "nearable" sensors can track ON/OFF-medication gait changes in a home-like setting.

Type
First-author study · Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation, 2026
Role
First author — methods, analysis, writing
Cohort
15 mild PD (ON & OFF states) · 14 matched controls · 4 m walks, Living Lab
Methods
RGB-D skeleton tracking · UWB radar range-Doppler · ICC & Bland–Altman agreement · FDR-corrected group tests
Tools
Azure Kinect · UWB radar · SensFloor · Python (camera + stats) · MATLAB (radar)
Result
Stride time agrees across devices (r = 0.90); stride length separates OFF-state PD from controls, and radar separates ON from OFF.
03

Levodopa's effect on basal ganglia–cerebellum connectivity

Mapping how long-term levodopa reorganises resting-state motor connectivity in Parkinson's.

Type
First-author study · npj Parkinson's Disease, 2025
Role
First author — analysis and writing
Data
29 PPMI patients · drug-naïve → levodopa-medicated · resting-state fMRI
Methods
Seed-based & network connectivity · SVM + PCA classification (LOOCV) · Eigenvector Centrality Mapping · permutation testing
Tools
FreeSurfer / FS-FAST · SUIT & Destrieux atlases · Brain Connectivity Toolbox · MATLAB
Result
Basal ganglia–cerebellum network classifies medication state (AUC 0.73); effect localises to cerebellar Crus II hubs.
04

A compensatory role of the cerebellum in early Parkinson's

Separating disease-driven brain atrophy from healthy ageing across two longitudinal cohorts.

Type
First-author study · NeuroImage, 2025
Role
First author — full analysis
Data
56 early PD (PPMI) · 53 matched controls (OASIS-3) · longitudinal T1 MRI, 2–3 years apart
Methods
Longitudinal volumetry · FreeSurfer (longitudinal stream) · CERES cerebellar segmentation
Tools
FreeSurfer · CERES
Result
Cerebellar white matter is enlarged early, then atrophies fastest (Crus I, lobule IX) — a compensation signature.
